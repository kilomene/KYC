# Specimen dossier — Nigeria National Driver's Licence (FRSC)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Sources cited inline; **no real person's document was
used.** Items marked **UNVERIFIED** were not confirmed in an opened source.

> **Vintage warning:** the only detailed front/back specimen description in a
> public official-adjacent source is the **2007 (pre-2011 scheme)** card, with a
> **2012 update** listing what the 2011 scheme changed. The two vintages differ
> in photo side, barcode complexity and features. **Do not merge them into one
> template** — carry both, as §3/§6 set out.

## 1. Issuer, official name, production

- Issuer/designer/producer: **Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC)** — the only
  agency mandated by law to design and produce the licence; state authorities
  issue after FRSC production (FRSC SLA, 2nd ed. 2026:
  https://staging.frsc.gov.ng/frsc-admin/uploads/REVIEWED_SLA_2026_276a6818e9.pdf;
  IRB Canada 2012, quoting the Corps Marshal:
  https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1162149.html). The tripartite system is
  FRSC (design/production) / VIO (proficiency testing) / State Internal
  Revenue Service (pricing, payment, distribution).
- Official name: **National Driver's Licence** (portal title: "Nigeria Driver's
  Licence", https://nigeriadriverslicence.frsc.gov.ng/; FAQ/payment portal:
  https://pay.nigeriadriverslicence.org/faq).
- Production: historically centralised at FRSC HQ Abuja; current production is
  FRSC print farms (a Lagos mini print farm, inaugurated Feb 2025, is reported
  at 1,800 cards/hour–9,000 per six hours; Oct 2025 brought contactless capture
  and on-the-spot printing). **No named private printing partner was found in
  any opened source — UNVERIFIED.**
- Material: the 2007 specimen is described only as having a "laminate
  coating". **Polycarbonate is reported by a single secondary source for the
  Oct 2025 instant-print card only** (brandarena.com.ng) — treat as reported,
  not FRSC-published. No FRSC spec sheet states a substrate.

## 2. Physical format

- Dimensions: the 2007 specimen "measures **approximately 9.5 cm × 6 cm**"
  with rounded edges (IRB Canada 2007:
  https://www.irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=451457).
  The 2012 update says the new licence "is smaller than the old one" without
  dimensions. **ID-1 (85.6 × 54 mm) is UNVERIFIED** — the only sources stating
  it were fake-template sellers, excluded as non-reputable. Settle the current
  card's aspect ratio from the owner's specimen.
- Orientation: landscape is implied by the 9.5 × 6 cm measurement and stripes
  "across the top", but **no opened source states the word — treat as
  near-certain, not quoted fact.**

## 3. Front layout — by vintage

### 2007 specimen (IRB Canada, from samples shown by a Nigerian High Commission official)

- **Header:** across the top, a **green stripe**: line 1 "Federal Republic of
  Nigeria", line 2 "national drivers licence" (casing as quoted by IRB).
- **Coat of arms:** in the **background, centre** of the front. A Nigerian
  **flag on the front: UNVERIFIED** — no opened source places one.
- **Second stripe** below the green: **blue for private, red for commercial**,
  containing: **licence number (left)**, the word "private"/"commercial"
  (centre), **licence class (right)**.
- **Fields below the stripes** (presence confirmed; exact printed label
  strings mostly **UNVERIFIED** — IRB lists fields, not labels): issuing
  state of the current licence; issuing state of the first licence; issue
  date; expiry date; holder's name; address; blood group; facial marks; sex;
  glasses Y/N; date of birth; height; number of replacement cards; number of
  renewals. A 2005 Lagos State list adds thumb print and signature.
- **Photo: main portrait on the LEFT; a smaller "ghost" image in the bottom
  right corner; thumbprint above the ghost image.**
- **Bottom strip:** a **bar code at the bottom of the FRONT, below the
  photo**; date of first issue below the bar code; holder's signature to the
  right of the bar code; "authorized signatory" to the right of the
  signature; at bottom right, the word "endorse" followed by numbers
  (1), (2), (3)… (FRSC pre-issuance verifications).

### 2011 scheme changes (IRB Canada 2012, Issuing Commandant)

- Barcode "more complex".
- **Photo moved to the RIGHT side** of the front.
- **No thumbprint** on the updated licence.
- Coat of arms centred; a **smaller hologram of the coat of arms on the right
  hand side** of the card.
- A contemporary guide corroborates the number position persisting in the
  current card: licence number "on the left side of the coloured stripe at
  the top — blue stripe for private licences, red for commercial"
  (awajis.com). Photo side in that guide is not stated.

### Label wording

Exact printed abbreviations for name/DOB/sex/height/issue/expiry are
**UNVERIFIED** from opened sources. The one confirmed label string comes from
the back index (§6): **"BG: Blood Group"**. Do not template `L/NO`, `D of B`,
`ISS`, `EXP`, `F/MARKS` etc. as confirmed print — calibrate label anchors
from the owner's specimen.

## 4. Licence number format

- **Confirmed: 12 characters, a mix of letters and digits** ("typically 12
  characters including letters and digits" — fenixtelematics.ng verification
  guide; corroborated by awajis.com). Entry errors commonly confuse O/0 and
  I/1 — build that confusion into OCR corroboration.
- Position: front, **left of the coloured stripe** (§3).
- **UNVERIFIED — do not calibrate on it:** any positional segmentation of the
  12 characters (e.g. a 3+5+2+2 split, a "centre code" segment, a check
  digit). **No opened official or reputable source breaks the number into
  segments.** One 2005 statement says the old-scheme number "indicat[es]
  local government of issue", but which characters carry that code is not
  stated anywhere opened.
- **Confusion risk:** the vehicle **plate** format `ABC-123DE` (3 letters =
  LGA code) is documented for number plates — it is **not** the driver
  licence format and must not be transferred.
- Older formats: sources distinguish old vs new *schemes* (appearance and
  process, 2011 changeover) and state the old number "identifies the
  applicant and cannot be changed", but **no distinct old number pattern is
  published — UNVERIFIED.**

## 5. Validity and renewal

- **Term: 3 years or 5 years**, applicant's choice (5-year option added by
  the Joint Tax Board, April 2016; both run side-by-side — Punch/Vanguard
  reports of the JTB announcement; current portal FAQ confirms "either a
  3-year or 5-year validity", with class-differentiated fees).
- **Expiry anchored to the holder's birthday: historically documented,
  currently UNVERIFIED.** A 2003 account (Nigerian High Commission attaché)
  describes a licence expiring on "the day and month of her birth"; no FRSC
  portal/SLA page opened states a birthday rule for the current card, and a
  current secondary guide says expiry runs "three or five years **from
  issue**". **The accounts conflict — never hard-reject a card whose expiry
  is not a birthday anniversary.**
- Renewal: applications accepted **from 30 days before expiry** (portal);
  SLA wording "within one calendar month before expiration"; a driving test
  is required if 6+ months have elapsed since the person last held a valid
  licence; vision test and biometric capture at the DLC; temporary licence
  (60 days) in the standard process. Reissue (lost/stolen/defaced) requires
  a police extract + sworn affidavit + fee.

## 6. Back layout and barcode

Source for the back is the **2007 vintage** (IRB) unless noted:

- **Top strip: a non-magnetic brown strip across the top** — explicitly
  *non-magnetic* in that vintage, i.e. **not a mag stripe**. A magnetic
  stripe on any later vintage: **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Class definitions:** the back "provides a description of the driver's
  licence classes (class 'A' through 'J')".
- **Index:** the back provides "an index for the acronyms that appear on the
  front (e.g., 'BG: Blood Group')" — the back index is the authoritative key
  to the front's abbreviations; transcribe it in full from the specimen.
- A contemporary guide corroborates in outline only: back shows "the classes
  of driver's license, Index, and other information" (guidecrest.com.ng).
- **Barcode — symbology UNVERIFIED in opened sources; MEASURED on a
  genuine specimen 2026-10-27:** no official/reputable source names the
  symbology, but the owner's genuine 2011-scheme card's back code
  **decodes with zxing-cpp as PDF417 carrying an AAMVA-structured
  payload** ("@" / "ANSI" header, DL subfile: DAQ licence number,
  DCS full name packed into the surname element with DAC omitted,
  DCA class, DBD/DBB/DBA dates, DAG address). The scanner therefore
  runs the AAMVA parser on this type's back code and cross-checks
  it against the printed front — a measured fact about this
  specimen's vintage, not a published FRSC specification; other
  vintages may differ. Position by vintage: 2007 — front bottom (§3);
  2012 press — "barcodes at the back of the license… contain the bio-data of
  the holder" and are scannable by FRSC field operatives (247ureports).
  A QR code belongs to the **digital** licence (eNDL activation per the
  portal); a QR printed on the physical card's back is **UNVERIFIED**.

## 7. Licence classes

Current official list (FRSC portal FAQ, NDL 2.0): **A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H,
J** — note **I is skipped**; a licence may bear **at most three** class
denotations. Definitions (Nigeria Highway Code, classes page):

| Class | Covers |
|---|---|
| A | Motorcycle |
| B | Motor vehicles <3 tonnes gross weight, excl. motorcycles, taxis, stage carriages, omnibuses |
| C | Motor vehicles <3 tonnes gross weight, excl. motorcycles (the commercial light class) |
| D | Motor vehicles excl. motorcycles, taxis, stage carriages, omnibuses, and articulated vehicles drawing a trailer |
| E | Motor vehicles excl. motorcycles or articulated vehicles |
| F | Agricultural machines and tractors |
| G | Articulated vehicles |
| H | Earth-moving vehicles |
| J | Special category for physically handicapped individuals |
| V | Convoy driving (senior government / political office holders) — on the Highway Code page as a tenth class; **not** in the portal FAQ list |

Discrepancy flagged: older Highway Code text lists the special class as
**I**, not J — treat I as the older-code letter, J as current.

## 8. Security / originality features — photo visibility

From specimen/official-interview sources:

| Feature | Visible in an ordinary photo? |
|---|---|
| **Oval holograms** (2007: appear "depending on the angle at which it is hit by the light"); 2005 list: hologram, coat of arms, seal, laminate | Partially — angle-dependent sheen in a still; behaviour shows on tilt/video |
| **Coat-of-arms hologram**, right hand side (2011 scheme) | Partially, as above |
| **Ghost image** of the holder, front bottom-right (2007) | Yes |
| Central print farm + biometrics + "intelligent camera" as the anti-counterfeit *system* (2011 launch) | Process feature — not itself photo-checkable |

From secondary verification guides only (**reported, not FRSC-published** —
nigerianqueries.com / nigerianfinder.com): laser perforation (tiny holes
visible against light), ghost portrait, microprinting, guilloche design,
variable micro script, split fountain printing, overlapping data. Treat each
as a specimen-check item, not an established feature.

**UNVERIFIED — no opened source states these for this card:** OVI;
guilloche/microprint as FRSC-published features; an embedded chip on the
physical card.

## 9. Live-scan practicalities

- **Two vintages circulate** with different photo sides (left 2007, right
  2011) and different feature sets (thumbprint present/absent). The template
  must be vintage-aware; a right-side photo is not an anomaly.
- **Number check = shape only:** 12 characters, letters+digits, with O/0 and
  I/1 confusion expected in OCR. No segmentation, no check digit, no centre
  code — corroborate the number by re-reading the stripe zone, not by
  decoding it.
- **Barcode:** attempt decode wherever it appears (front-bottom on old,
  back on newer per press). On the measured 2011-scheme specimen the
  back code is PDF417 with an AAMVA-structured payload (§6) — decode
  and cross-check it; other vintages are unmeasured, so a failed
  PDF417 decode is not evidence of forgery on this type.
- **Expiry:** read the printed EXP; never compute from issue + 3/5 years and
  never require a birthday anniversary (§5).
- Wear: field licences are often heavily worn/faded — the stripe (number,
  class) and the name block are the most durable zones.
