KYC

Know Your Customer, built our way. An identity verification platform for businesses that need to confirm a person is who they claim to be — before giving them an account, money, or access.

Live at kyc.novamail.store

What is live

  • Tenant API keys with strict test/live separation
  • Phone-only hosted flow with a live camera document scanner
  • Automatic capture — the scanner must detect a real document first
  • Automatic decisions in the standard path
  • Document classification, multi-pass OCR, MRZ and barcode cross-checks
  • Continuous face tracking, face match and active liveness
  • Screen/forgery evidence, deepfake signals, VPN/proxy hard gate
  • Proof of address, sanctions screening and ongoing monitoring
  • KYB business verification and reusable verified credentials
  • Review console for audit, exceptions and required dispositions

Honest limits

  • KYB has no live government registry connection yet — it is format validation, document evidence and reviewer confirmation, labelled as such.
  • Liveness is an active movement challenge. It is not passive liveness and carries no iBeta or ISO certification.
  • Face and liveness accuracy benchmarks (FAR/FRR) are Phase 4 work; no benchmark number is claimed yet.
  • Webhook delivery is one signed attempt, logged. Retries and replay are not built yet.
  • The service is reached through a Cloudflare tunnel; final Cloudflare-native hosting is Phase 4 work.

Access

Tenant teams work cases in the review console. Applicant verification links are created per session by a tenant through the API and look like /flow/<token>.
