How it works

One hosted flow on the applicant's phone — your product creates a session, sends the person to it, and receives the outcome. Desktop visitors are handed off to their phone by QR code automatically.

1

Create a session

Your server creates an applicant and a verification session through the API, and receives a private flow link that expires on its own.

2

The person scans

Live camera only — the document is captured automatically when it fills the frame, with zoom and four-corner framing. Voice guidance talks the person through every step, in a natural human voice.

3

Selfie + liveness

A selfie is matched against the portrait printed on the document, with continuous face tracking; then a server-issued movement challenge proves a live person — not a photo, not a replay.

4

Decision, delivered

The engine decides automatically — approved, rejected, or flagged with reasons. Your server learns the outcome instantly by signed webhook, or by polling the API.

Capabilities

Every layer below runs on every verification at its level. Nothing is a placeholder: if a check cannot be performed, the platform fails closed and says why — it never guesses a pass.

📷

Live document scanner

Camera-only capture (no gallery uploads, except the regulated NIN-slip path), fully automatic shutter, orientation freedom, and reads that must agree across multiple independent passes before they are believed.

🪪

Document-sourced identity

Per-country, per-document templates extract the name, number, dates and portrait; MRZ and barcode payloads cross-check the printed text. Disagreement means rejection, not a shrug.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Face verification

The live selfie is compared with the document's own portrait while a face track holds identity continuity across the whole capture — a swap mid-flow breaks the check.

🎭

Active liveness

Server-issued head-movement challenges, analysed for genuine motion and presentation-attack signals; the harness is evaluated against ISO-style PAD metrics.

🕸️

Fraud intelligence

Duplicate documents and faces are detected across attempts using privacy-preserving hashed indexes — raw values are never stored for matching — alongside velocity and coordinated-abuse signals.

🛡️

Screening & risk

Sanctions and watchlist screening, VPN/proxy hard-blocking with IP-country cross-checks against the document, and a risk engine that prices every signal into the decision.

🏢

Business verification

KYB records with people and documents, plus authoritative registry checks where a live registry source is connected — and an honest “not configured” where one is not.

🎫

Reusable credentials

An approved verification can issue a signed, claims-only credential the person can present to other businesses on the platform — proof without re-sharing documents.

🏠

Proof of address

Level-2 flows add a live-scanned proof-of-address document, checked for recency and consistency with the verified identity.

Built to be trusted — and to prove it

Trust is not a slogan here; it is the engineering posture of the whole platform.

How your data is protected

  • Encrypted at rest. Every stored document and biometric capture is AES-256-GCM encrypted, with a sealed, recoverable master-key backup.
  • Secrets never sit in the open. API keys and webhook secrets are hashed or shown exactly once; tenant data is isolated and cross-tenant access is tested to 404.
  • Erasure is real. A subject-erasure request deletes stored media and nulls personal fields, and records that it did so.

How decisions stay honest

  • Fail closed, always. Missing capability, unreadable data, or a provider that is not configured produces a refusal with a named reason — never a silent pass.
  • A complete audit trail. Every meaningful action — reads, decisions, deletions — is written to an audit log that survives even tenant removal.
  • Honest health. The status on this page reads the platform's real capability-aware health endpoint — if a critical engine is down, it says so. Watch it in the menu.

For developers

Two keys per tenant (test and live), a hosted flow so you never touch raw biometrics, signed webhooks, a zero-dependency JavaScript SDK, and a complete OpenAPI 3.1 specification for websites, mobile apps, backends and AI agents. Test mode runs the full pipeline and is always free.

Questions, answered plainly

Why are there no name and date-of-birth forms?

Because typed identity is the weakest link in verification — people mistype, and fraudsters type whatever they like. Nova KYC reads identity from the document itself and asks the person only to confirm what was read. If a read is wrong, the correction path is a better scan, not an edit box.

What does the person being verified need?

A phone with a camera and their physical identity document. The flow is phone-first by design; someone who opens the link on a desktop is shown a QR code to continue on their phone. Voice guidance is built into the flow itself.

What happens when a scan is bad?

The flow coaches in real time — face presence, framing, lighting, and a closer scan when the document's printed photo is small — and the read-back shows exactly what was read before anything is submitted. Decisions name their reasons, and capture-quality failures come with plain retry guidance.

Where do documents and selfies go?

They are stored encrypted on the platform, visible only to the tenant that collected them, and never travel in webhooks or credentials (those carry claims and outcomes only). Subject erasure deletes the media and nulls the personal fields.

Is it automatic, or does a human review?

Automatic. The standard path has no manual review: the engine approves genuine verifications and rejects invalid ones with named reason codes. Cases the engine elevates carry their signals with them for the tenant's own review console.
