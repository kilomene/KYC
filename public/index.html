KYC

Know Your Customer, built our way. A Nova Inc product.

Status — honest

Phase 1 local MVP. This build runs locally: tenant API keys, hosted applicant flow (consent → document → selfie → submit), real checks (image quality, OCR, MRZ check digits, expiry/age, name/DOB consistency), a fail-closed decision engine, and human review in the console.

Not yet built: face match and liveness are Phase 2 — so Level 2/3 sessions cannot auto-approve here; they go to a human reviewer who compares the document and selfie images. Proof of address, sanctions screening, and KYB are Phase 3. Nothing on this page claims a certification we do not hold.
