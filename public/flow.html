Before we start — your consent

We will collect: your name and date of birth, a live camera scan of your government ID, and a live selfie. Higher verification levels also ask for a short live liveness video and a scan of a proof-of-address document. We also record your device type and your IP address, and use the IP address for a location check against the country on your application. VPN and proxy connections are not accepted. This is used only to verify your identity for the business that sent you here. A human reviewer may look at your document and selfie images side by side. You can ask that business to delete your data. Nothing is collected until you agree.

A camera is required

Live verification needs your camera. It cannot be done with photos from your gallery — the scan has to happen live, here, so we know the document and the person are really present.

Scan your ID document

That is all you choose. You never type your name, date of birth or document number — the scanner reads them from the document itself, and shows you what it read before you continue.

The NIN slip is laminated paper — a live scan reads it badly, so for this document only you upload a photo of the slip and type your NIN. Everything else is unchanged: your name is still read from the slip itself, and your selfie is still a live camera check, matched against the photo printed on the slip. The number on the slip must match the number you type.

Scan the FRONT of your document

Hold your document inside the frame. The scanner has to see your ID — its edges and shape — before it can take the picture, and it takes it automatically the moment everything is right. There is no button to press.
