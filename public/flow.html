Before we start — your consent

We will collect: your name and date of birth, a photo of your government ID, and a selfie. Higher verification levels also ask for a short liveness video and a proof-of-address document. This is used only to verify your identity for the business that sent you here. A human reviewer may look at your document and selfie images side by side. You can ask that business to delete your data. Nothing is collected until you agree.

3. Selfie

Take a clear selfie in good light. It is compared automatically with the photo on your document, and a human reviewer can also compare them side by side.

4. Liveness check — active liveness (movement challenge)

We will ask you to move your head in two directions shown on screen, while recording a short video (about 4 seconds). A still photo or a replayed recording will not pass. You can also upload a short video file instead.

5. Proof of address

Upload a recent utility bill, bank statement or official letter showing your name and address. It must be dated within the last 90 days.
