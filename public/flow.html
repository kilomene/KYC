Verify your identity

Before we start — your consent

We will collect: your name and date of birth, a live camera scan of your government ID, and a live selfie. Higher verification levels also ask for a short live liveness video and a scan of a proof-of-address document. This is used only to verify your identity for the business that sent you here. A human reviewer may look at your document and selfie images side by side. You can ask that business to delete your data. Nothing is collected until you agree.

A camera is required

Live verification needs your camera. It cannot be done with photos from your gallery — the scan has to happen live, here, so we know the document and the person are really present.
